# 🚢 Titanic Survival Prediction & Analysis

## 📑 Executive Summary & Dataset Facts
* **Sample Size:** Evaluates a simplified educational dataset of **891 passengers** out of the 2,200+ individuals aboard the RMS Titanic.
* **Gender Survival Split:** **74.2% of female passengers** survived compared to only **18.9% of male passengers**.
* **Socioeconomic Class Impact:** Survival rates scaled directly with ticket class—**62.9% for 1st Class**, **47.8% for 2nd Class**, and **24.2% for 3rd Class**.
* **Tech Stack:** **Python** (Pandas, Scikit-Learn for Machine Learning models), **Data Exploration & Classification**.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement / Core Goal
Build, train, and evaluate supervised classification machine learning models using a data analyst approach to predict passenger survival outcomes based on demographic factors, ticket class, and gender.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights & Machine Learning Findings
* **Gender as the Primary Predictor:** Female passengers had a significantly higher survival likelihood due to historical evacuation protocols ("women and children first").
* **Class & Fare Influence:** Higher ticket tiers provided superior proximity to lifeboats and earlier evacuation access, directly doubling the survival rates of 1st Class passengers relative to 3rd Class.
* **Model Takeaway:** Feature engineering around gender, passenger class, and age yields the highest predictive accuracy when training supervised binary classification models.
